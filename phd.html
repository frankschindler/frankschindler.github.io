Thank you for your interest in doing a PhD with me. I am currently looking for up to 2 PhD students to start in the autumn of 2027.

I have direct funding for 1 PhD student who has home fee status. There is a slight chance that this position can be upgraded to cover overseas fees in exceptional cases.

Furthermore, I can support 1 further student (irrespective of fee status) in applying for a President's PhD scholarship. There are 3 alternative deadlines for this fellowship, but the first one (in November) offers the best chances of success.

How to apply

Because Imperial's application system can be slow and unwieldy, I'd like to do a fairly minimal preselection step before recommending you to apply formally. For this, please send me your transcripts and CV via email by the following deadlines:

  • 25 Oct 2026, if you'd like to be considered for a President's scholarship and the funded position
  • 3 Jan 2027, if you are only going for the funded position

After these deadlines, I'll invite you to a short interview taking place in the same week. You don't need reference letters for the pre-selection, but please make sure they are ready by the deadlines in case we want to go ahead with the formal process.
